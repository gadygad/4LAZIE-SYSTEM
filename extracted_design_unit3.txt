Campus Network Design  
 Definition 
A Campus Network is a high-speed network that connects multiple buildings within a 
limited geographical area such as a college, university, office, or hospital. 
It is designed to provide fast, secure, and scalable communication between different 
departments and users. 
Simple Idea 
A campus network connects: 
 Computer Labs  
 Library  
 Administration Office  
 Departments  
 Hostels  
 Staff Rooms  
All are connected through a central backbone network.

2️⃣ Distribution Layer 
This layer acts as a middle layer between access and core. 
Functions: 
 Inter-VLAN routing  
 Policy enforcement  
 Traffic filtering  
 Load balancing  
 
3️⃣ Core Layer 
This is the backbone of the network. 
Functions: 
 High-speed data transfer  
 Fast switching  
 Reliable backbone connectivity  
 Minimal processing for speed  
Design Objectives 
 1. Scalability 
Network should support future expansion. 
 2. Security 
Prevent unauthorized access using VLANs and firewalls. 
 3. High Performance 
Ensure fast data transfer with minimal delay. 
 4. Reliability 
Network should be stable and always available.

里 VLAN in Campus Network 
VLANs divide the network logically:

A WAN (Wide Area Network) is a network that connects computers and networks over a 
large geographical area such as cities, countries, or continents. 
 Example: 
 Internet is the largest WAN  
 Bank networks connecting branches  
 Company offices in different countries  
易 Simple Definition 
WAN connects multiple LANs (Local Area Networks) across long distances using service 
providers. 
 
WAN Characteristics 
 1. Large Coverage Area 
 City to city  
 Country to country  
 Global communication  
 2. Uses Service Providers 
 ISP (Internet Service Provider)  
 Telecom companies  
 3. High Cost 
 Expensive compared to LAN
