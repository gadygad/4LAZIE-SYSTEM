Privacy Policy

Last updated: Today

1. Introduction

Welcome to 4LAZIE ("we", "us", or "our"). By visiting or using our platform, you agree to our Terms and Privacy Policy. Our goal is to provide an excellent and accessible platform for student resources.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information such as your name, email address, and academic details when you register for an account. We also collect data on how you interact with our platform to improve our services and provide relevant notes and past papers.

3. Cookies & Similar Technologies

We use a small number of cookies to run the platform: a session cookie to keep you signed in, a CSRF-protection cookie to keep forms secure, and — only if you tick "Keep me signed in" — a longer-lived cookie so you don't have to log in every visit. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies. If your browser or device supports it, we also use a service worker to cache pages for faster loading and basic offline access (a Progressive Web App feature) — this only stores site files on your device and does not track you across other websites.

4. Third-Party Services

To run 4LAZIE, we share limited data with a small number of trusted service providers, strictly to provide the platform's core features:

  • Google Sign-In — if you choose to register or log in with Google, Google shares your name, email, and profile picture with us to create your account. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Cloudinary — uploaded files (notes, profile pictures, past papers) are stored with Cloudinary, our file-hosting provider, so they can be reliably served to students.
  • Gmail (SMTP) — account verification, password-reset, and security-alert emails are sent through Gmail's mail servers.
  • Web push notifications — if you enable notifications, your browser generates a push subscription (not tied to your identity beyond your account) that we use only to deliver notifications you'd otherwise see in-app.

We do not sell your personal data to anyone, for any purpose.

5. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our platform.
  • Enhance your experience (e.g., showing courses and materials relevant to you).
  • Communicate with you regarding updates or support.

6. User Content

Notes, inline forum comments, or any documents you upload remain your property, but by uploading, you grant us a license to display and share them with other 4LAZIE students in accordance with our platform's rules. If a note you upload is marked as covering a subject taught in more than one course, it may also be shown to students in those other courses at the same academic level and semester, so students studying the same subject can benefit from it regardless of which course uploaded it.

7. Student Profiles, Messaging & Connections

Your name, course/program, profile picture, bio (if provided), and any Verified Badges are visible to other registered students when they view your profile. Registered students can send you direct messages, reply to specific messages, and see basic presence information (such as whether you are online and message read status). You can also "Connect" with (follow) other students — your Connections count (how many students follow you) is visible on your profile, and who you follow is used only to power the Connect/Connections feature. We do not share this information with anyone outside the platform.

8. Content Moderation & Reporting

You can report a forum post or comment that violates our community guidelines. Reported content stays visible until an administrator reviews it — reporting does not automatically hide anything. To help administrators spot recurring problems, the platform automatically flags an account for admin review after it receives multiple formal warnings or multiple upheld reports; this flag is visible only to administrators and is used solely to support moderation decisions, not shared publicly or with other students.

9. Exam Generator Hub

Our platform provides tools for generating exam templates (e.g., UE, CATs) for specific universities. These generated PDFs are intended strictly for academic use. Authorized administrators are responsible for ensuring that the contents of the generated exams align with their respective university's standards and regulations.

10. Security

Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed with bcrypt before being saved. Password-reset codes (OTPs), login attempts, and other sensitive account actions are rate-limited per network to make automated guessing impractical. We use HTTPS to encrypt data in transit, and access to administrative tools is restricted to authorized admin accounts only. No system is perfectly secure, but we actively monitor and improve these protections.

11. Data Retention & Your Rights

We keep your account information for as long as your account is active. You can update your profile details, bio, and social links at any time from your profile settings. If you would like your account or personal data deleted, contact the 4LAZIE support team at support@4lazie.com and we will act on your request, subject to any content already shared with other students (such as public forum posts) that may need to remain for platform integrity.

12. Fair Use

The resources available on 4LAZIE, including past papers and notes, are strictly for academic purposes. Misuse or any attempt to compromise our platform will result in immediate account termination.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.

14. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the 4LAZIE support team at support@4lazie.com.
